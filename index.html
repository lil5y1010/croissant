The Art of Baking Croissants

Croissants are buttery, flaky pastries originally associated with French baking. They are made from layers of dough and butter, which create their light, crispy texture. Baking croissants takes patience and care, but the result is a delicious golden pastry perfect for breakfast or a snack.

How to Make Croissants

Ingredients

  • 500g all-purpose flour
  • 250ml milk
  • 50g sugar
  • 10g salt
  • 7g dry yeast
  • 250g cold butter
  • 1 egg for brushing

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, milk, sugar, salt, and yeast to make a dough.
  2. Chill the dough, then wrap it around the cold butter.
  3. Fold and roll the dough several times to create layers.
  4. Cut the dough into triangles and roll them into croissant shapes.
  5. Let the croissants rise until they become puffy.
  6. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 200°C until golden brown.

Nutrition Facts (per 100 g)

  • Calories: 406 kcal
  • Protein: 8 g
  • Carbohydrates: 45 g
  • Fat: 21 g
